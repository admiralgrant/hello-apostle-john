A Prayer for You

Father, we lift up Your servant, Apostle John. Let Your face shine upon him and be gracious to him; lift up Your countenance upon him and give him peace. Renew his strength like the eagle’s. Where he is weary, carry him; where he has poured out, fill him again to overflowing.

Guard his heart with the peace that surpasses all understanding, and let goodness and mercy follow him all the days of his life. May the works of his hands prosper, and may his latter days be greater than the former.

Amen

With love and thanksgiving,

— from a grateful heart
